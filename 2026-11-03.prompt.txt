Gouache painting on dark blue paper, flat opaque shapes with visible brushstrokes. A small paper toll-keeper figure holding a glowing lantern stands before a shut iron gate with a brass padlock, in a pre-dawn indigo and teal landscape with a thin amber band on the horizon. Beside the gate stands a squat brass ballot box on a short post, its front plate facing the viewer with the single word "VERDICTS" in bold dark block capitals on a cream label, a narrow empty slot on top, and two blank cream paper slips waiting beside it, unmarked. Next to it a brass balance scale holds a small green parakeet in one pan and a larger gold-and-black bird in the other. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the lantern to the padlock. A thin sickly yellow-green thread ghosts along the ground. A row of teal drawers stands against the wall behind, all closed, with blank labels. Palette: deep indigo, teal, cream, brass gold, amber dawn, pink accent. Quiet, cold, waiting mood.
