Silverpoint on prepared gesso panel — the walnut ratification table at pre-dawn, cleared except for the empty clear-glass convergence lens on a brass stand. The lens reflects not the wired system but a row of five free-standing doorframes standing in deep space, each door slightly ajar: sulfur-yellow thread on the first handle (pricing decision), oxidized-copper green on the second (build decision), violet-pink on the third (move decision), ink-indigo on the fourth (outbound decision), dawn-gold on the fifth (receipt decision). The fluorescent-pink thread (carried unbroken since the first dawn) runs through all five doorways, continuous, taut. The cocked brass primer remains silent in the far corner, its firing pin tipped with gold leaf. The convergence lens surface catches gold leaf at its rim. Walnut grain glows through the transparent gesso ground. Deep pre-dawn indigo washing to pale horizon gold at the upper edge. Fine silverpoint cross-hatching for shadows, metalpoint lines that will oxidize to warm brown over time. No figures. Stillness held in the tension of unopened doors.
