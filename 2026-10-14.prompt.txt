Oil painting on linen, thick impasto palette-knife strokes, visible canvas weave: a small paper toll-keeper figure holding a brass lantern stands on a teal-shadowed ridge at first light, beside a newly built brass switchboard-kiosk with ten round brass sockets in two rows, each socket holding a plugged cord; one cord glows. From the kiosk a single fluorescent-pink thread runs across the ridge to a cocked brass primer on a rock in the far corner. Three signal lanterns on posts stand below, dim, steady amber, cold white. A faint sickly yellow-green thread lies at the base of the kiosk. The kiosk and all papers and tags are blank or sparse abstract strokes, no readable writing anywhere. Palette: deep teal, rust, cream, dawn amber, brass gold, one pink accent. Quiet, hopeful, landscape composition.
