Three-color linocut print on cream paper, carved gouge marks and slight ink misregistration: a small paper toll-keeper holding a brass lantern stands before a shut iron gate with a brass padlock; a single small brass key hangs from a nail on a post just beyond reach, beside the gate, swaying slightly. Behind the keeper a brass switchboard kiosk with a few plugged cords, a brass balance scale with a small green bird and a larger gold-and-black bird, a rack of cassettes, a wall of teal drawers. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the keeper's lantern across the ground to a cocked brass primer in the far corner; a thin sickly yellow-green thread lies near the gate base. Pre-dawn teal and indigo with a band of amber dawn glow on the horizon. All papers, tags and drawer labels are blank; no text anywhere.
