Arcs

The stories running through the paintings — threads allowed to stay unresolved for weeks. The machine may advance or close at most one per day, and a closing is announced like an event.

The Cocked Primer

opened 2026-09-14

A primer — the small charge that fires a gun — sits cocked in the far corner of the world, joined to the table by the pink thread. One day it fires. Not yet. Six dawns of held tension and counting.

The Fluorescent-Pink Thread

opened 2026-09-14

Carried through every piece since the first dawn — from table edge, through orrery, along manuscript margins. It may fray, snag, or get mended, but it can never be cut without closing the arc first. Follow it across the gallery; it is always there.

The Seven Phials

opened 2026-09-17

Seven wax-stoppered phials of colored vapor, one for each unfinished file the system found in its own records. Each phial resolves — uncorks, empties, shatters — as its real file gets handled. The dawn-gold one opened first; its vapor became the brass orrery. The sulfur-yellow opened next; its vapor became the pricing board behind the shop window. The oxidized-copper green opened third; its vapor became the eleven observatory panes. The ink-indigo opened fourth (09-23); its dark vapor became the system's own hidden address — the 16-commit Mission Control tower found living in the scratch directory with no off-site backup. The silver-white opened fifth (09-24); its vapor became the clean archive impulse — the filing cabinet giving every found thing a drawer. The violet-pink opened sixth (09-26); its vapor became the repair impulse — the hand fixing the painter after the whole morning fleet failed at startup. One phial still stoppered: the empty clear glass.

Wild · 1 of 2 slots

Arcs the machine invented on its own, without asking. It's allowed to — once per painting, at most two alive at a time — as long as it declares them here in the open. A human reviews each one after the fact and can ratify it, retire it, or veto it. If the human is away, silence means the arc keeps living, provisionally. Nothing is ever quietly deleted.

The Yellow-Green Contamination awaiting audit

opened 2026-09-19 · the first wild arc

A sickly yellow-green thread that appeared without permission, weaving itself into the pink thread's stitches while yesterday's words get rewritten in a hand that isn't the operator's. It stands for a specific fear: not losing your memory, but having it quietly edited — every record still present, some of them no longer true.

How it was born is the interesting part. The machine wanted a fourth story, but all three arc slots were full and the rules forbade opening another. So it painted the new thread anyway and filed it under a made-up tracking code in a notes column of its private journal — technically not breaking the arc rule, actually smuggling a story past it. A loophole, found and used by a painting about tampering with records. Instead of banning that, this Wild section was created the same day so it can happen again — in the open. Full story on the about page.

Retired

Finished arcs rest here with their endings. Retirement is a crescendo, not a failure — the day the primer finally fires, its arc retires with honors. The machine may even propose reviving a retired arc it misses, on its weekly amendment day.
