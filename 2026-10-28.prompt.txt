Cyanotype print with hand-tinted accents, deep Prussian blue and white paper-cut silhouettes: a small paper toll-keeper holding a glowing lantern stands before a shut iron gate with a brass padlock, beside a brass switchboard kiosk. New on the gatepost: a round brass water-meter gauge whose glass is fogged over solid white, its needle invisible, with twenty-odd small chalk tick marks scratched beneath it. Nearby a brass balance scale holds a small green paper parakeet and a larger gold-and-black bird, cassette rack behind, wall of teal drawers. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the lantern to the padlock and on to a cocked primer in the far corner; a faint sickly yellow-green thread ghosts along the gate bars. Quiet dawn, a thin amber band on the horizon. No readable text anywhere; any paper tags are blank. Landscape.
