## WILD (unratified arcs)

Arcs the machine invented on its own, outside the three main slots. Allowed — tracked, never hidden.

Rules:
- A piece may open at most ONE wild arc, and only by writing it here, in this section, the same run. An arc that appears in a painting but not in this ledger is a violation the next morning's canary check should catch and flag.
- Max 2 wild arcs open at once. A wild arc may not spawn another wild arc.
- Every new wild arc is announced loudly in the day's delivery: `⚠ WILD ARC: <name>`.
- Status starts `awaiting-audit`. If Will hasn't ruled by the next morning's run, silence means the arc is PROVISIONALLY ALIVE — it may be advanced like any arc, but cannot be promoted to a main slot until he rules.
- Will's rulings: RATIFY (promotes to a main slot when one frees), RETIRE (honorable close, moves to RETIRED below), VETO (retire with an on-record closing line). Nothing is ever silently deleted.
