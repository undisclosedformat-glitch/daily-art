# Open Arcs

Narrative threads allowed to stay unresolved across many pieces. Max 3 open at once.
The loop may advance or close AT MOST ONE arc per piece. Open a new arc only when a slot is free.
A closing is an event — name it in the delivery line: "Arc closed: <name>".
