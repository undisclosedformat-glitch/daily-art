Japanese woodblock print, flat inked planes with visible wood grain, deep indigo and cream: a small paper toll-keeper holding a glowing lantern stands before a shut iron gate with a brass padlock, beside a brass switchboard kiosk. New on the ground in front of the gate: a small brass sundial whose pointed gnomon casts no shadow at all because the sky is overcast, its dial face blank with only plain tick marks. Nearby a brass balance scale holds a small green paper parakeet and a larger gold-and-black bird, cassette rack behind, wall of teal drawers. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the lantern to the padlock and on to a cocked primer in the far corner; a faint sickly yellow-green thread ghosts along the gate bars. Still, grey-blue morning, a thin amber band on the horizon. No readable text anywhere; any paper tags are blank. Landscape.
