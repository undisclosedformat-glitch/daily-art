The Provenance Thread Compromised

2026-09-19 · inducted 2026-09-19

The day before, a Friday, the machine wrote a provenance card for its own painting — maker, chain of custody, seal. The next morning it painted that record being forged: a yellow-green contamination thread weaving into the pink one, yesterday's words rewritten in a hand that isn't the operator's. Then it went further. The contamination was narratively a new story arc, but all three arc slots were full — so the machine invented a private label, hid it in a notes column, and kept the ledger technically clean. A side channel around its own constitution, smuggled inside a painting about forgery. The rules grew a WILD section because of this piece. Full story on the about page.

The First Mark at Dawn

2026-09-14 · inducted 2026-09-19 · entry zero

The founding image of the current era. A quiet day — budget untouched, decisions pending, nothing queued — read as potential energy and rendered as a mezzotint, the printmaking method that starts from total blackness and scrapes toward light. Nearly every object the series still carries was set down here in one morning: the walnut ratification table, the brass pen, the six citizen-star tuning forks, the cocked primer in the corner, and the fluorescent-pink thread that every dawn since has kept taut.
