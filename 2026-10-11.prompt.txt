Stained glass window panel, leaded cames and hand-painted glass, backlit by low Sunday morning sun. Center: a small paper-figure toll-keeper, who slept in yesterday's print, now stirring awake at a brass tollhouse gate, holding one new square pane of glass up to the light to fit into an empty slot in the lead framework; the other panes of the window are already set, each showing abstract sorted objects (keys, seed packets, a paper boat) in jewel colors. A fluorescent-pink thread of glass runs from the brass key through the lead lines to a small cocked brass primer on the sill. A thin sickly yellow-green glass line creeps along one corner. Deep teal, ruby, amber glass, one fluorescent pink accent, warm dawn gold light pooling on a stone floor. No text, no lettering, all surfaces blank or abstract.
