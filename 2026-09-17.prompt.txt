An oil painting on cracked gesso panel of the same walnut ratification table at mid-morning, the brass pen now resting in a grooved leather pen-rest, its nib still inked from the first mark. The fluorescent-pink thread (carried from the mezzotint dawn) stretches taut from the table's live edge to a cocked primer in shadow, but now seven delicate copper-wire filaments branch from it like roots, each terminating in a tiny glass phial stoppered with wax -- each phial holds a different colored vapor: acid-green (Feature-Manifest), indigo (Master-Index), burnt-umber (Open-Questions), verdigris (Pipeline-Map), bone-white (Session-Log), mercury-silver (memory snapshot), hematite-red (probe-confirmations). The walnut grain glows warm through thin glazes; dust motes drift in a shaft of pale gold light crossing the table at a low angle. The brass pen catches a highlight on its knurled grip. The primer's brass casing is dulled by age. Impasto texture on the wire filaments and phial stoppers; smooth glazes on the walnut and brass. Palette: warm walnut umbers, aged brass golds, seven distinct vapor hues against a ground of lead-white and raw umber, the fluorescent-pink thread a single electric accent.
