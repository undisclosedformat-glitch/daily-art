Oil pastel drawing on dark indigo paper, thick waxy strokes with visible paper tooth. A small paper toll-keeper figure holding a lantern stands before a shut iron gate with a brass padlock at pre-dawn. Newly added: a brass gramophone horn on a short post beside the gate, and next to it a small wax cylinder; from the horn's mouth a pale ribbon of sound curls out, and the ribbon's shape is a slightly wrong copy of the keeper's own silhouette, a thin imitation with stiff outline. A single amber dawn band glows along the horizon. Beside the figure a brass balance scale at rest holds a small green bird on one pan and a larger gold-and-black bird on the other. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the lantern to the padlock and off toward a small cocked brass primer in the far corner. A thin sickly yellow-green line ghosts along the gate bars. Closed teal drawers line the wall behind. Quiet, waiting mood, deep teal and indigo with cream and brass gold. No readable text anywhere; any tags or papers are blank.
