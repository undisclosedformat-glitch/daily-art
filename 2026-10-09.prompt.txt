Japanese woodblock print (mokuhanga), visible wood grain and layered flat color: a walnut table at dawn seen at a slight angle, where yesterday's sorted found objects (brass keys, seed packets, a paper boat, a film spool) now rest in four small lacquer trays. Beside them stands a tiny brass tollhouse with one warm lit window, built right on the table, with four narrow rails of different colors running through its gate like model lanes, and a small lever hand-cranked by a paper figure. A single fluorescent-pink thread ties a brass key to a cocked brass primer in the far corner; a faint sickly yellow-green thread ghosts the table edge. Pale dawn light from the upper left. Palette: indigo, cream, brass gold, one saturated pink accent, soft dawn gold. No readable text anywhere; all labels and tags are blank paper.
