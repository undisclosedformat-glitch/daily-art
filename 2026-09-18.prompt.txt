A single fluorescent-pink thread (continuous from previous dawns) passes through the orrery's plane, vibrating at the gear-mesh frequency, the only bright color in the shadowed scene. Pale dawn beam enters from upper left, catching the orrery's brass teeth and the open phial's rim, leaving the six stoppered phials as dark silhouettes. Tempera luminosity: optical mixing through translucent cross-hatched layers, gesso ground glowing through thin pigment, drypoint-fine detail on gear teeth and sphere reflections. Mood: dark-with-dawn stillness — tension held in the vibrating thread, the orrery's inevitable motion, six vapors waiting their turn.
