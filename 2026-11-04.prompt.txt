Two-color risograph print in misregistered teal and fluorescent-pink inks with brass-gold spot color on cream stock, visible halftone grain. A small paper toll-keeper figure holding a lantern stands before a shut iron gate with a brass padlock at pre-dawn. Newly added: a hand bell hung beside the gate on a short rope, its clapper tied down with a loop of cloth so it cannot ring; a single amber dawn band glows along the horizon. Beside the figure a brass balance scale at rest holds a small green bird on one pan and a larger gold-and-black bird on the other. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the lantern to the padlock and off toward a small cocked brass primer in the far corner. A thin sickly yellow-green line ghosts along the gate bars. Closed teal drawers line the wall behind. Quiet, waiting mood. No readable text anywhere; any tags or papers are blank.
