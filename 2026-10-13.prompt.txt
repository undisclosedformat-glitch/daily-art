Layered paper-cut shadow-box diorama, photographed straight on with soft raking light: a small paper toll-keeper in a cream coat holding a brass lantern stands on the far side of a rope footbridge, having crossed a misty teal gorge, on a ridge where three signal lanterns sit in a row on stone posts — the first lantern dimmed, the second glowing steady amber, the third small and cold-white waiting its turn. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the keeper's hand along the ridge to a small cocked brass primer on a rock. A faint sickly yellow-green thread hangs on the last post. Layers of deep teal, rust and cream paper with visible cut edges and depth shadows, pale dawn-amber glow behind the ridge. No writing anywhere; all tags are blank paper.
