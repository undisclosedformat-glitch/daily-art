Woven wool tapestry, Gobelin style with visible warp threads and slightly uneven selvedge: a small paper toll-keeper figure with a brass lantern stands on a rope footbridge of three successive spans over a misty teal gorge at first light, checking each span's knots on a Monday inspection round. The first span is thick and new, the second thinner, the third a frayed hemp strand that still holds. A single fluorescent-pink thread runs along the handrail to a cocked brass spring-loaded primer on the far cliff edge. A thin sickly yellow-green thread snags at one post. No writing anywhere; all tags and signs are blank woven cloth. Palette: deep teal, wool-cream, brass gold, rust, pale dawn amber, with the pink thread the only saturated accent.
