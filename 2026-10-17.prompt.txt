Long-exposure night photograph of a handmade miniature scene on a ridge: a small paper toll-keeper figure in a trench coat sits on an upturned crate holding a brass lantern, its light smeared into a soft warm streak by the long exposure. Beside him a brass switchboard kiosk with plugged cords, and a wooden rack of verdict stamps with dry cream-coloured ink pads. A single pale moth rests on one dry pad, its wings slightly blurred. Wooden bins of brass keys, a folded paper boat and film spools stand nearby. A fluorescent-pink thread runs along the ground from a brass key to a cocked brass primer in the far corner; a faint sickly yellow-green thread lies near the kiosk base. Star trails wheel over a deep teal sky, with a thin amber dawn line on the horizon. All paper tags and surfaces are blank, no writing anywhere. Quiet Saturday stillness, deep teal and plum shadows, brass gold highlights, shallow depth of field, film grain.
