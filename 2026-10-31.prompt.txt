Sumi-e ink wash painting on rough cream washi paper, loose black and grey brush washes with a few flat accents of colour. A small paper toll-keeper figure holding a lantern stands before a shut iron gate with a brass padlock at dawn. On the gatepost sits a hollowed carved turnip lantern with a keyhole-shaped mouth, lit from inside with warm orange light, like a Halloween mask put on the gate. Beside the gate a brass balance scale holds a small green paper parakeet in one pan and a larger gold-and-black bird in the other. A row of teal drawers in the wall behind. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the lantern's flame to a small cocked primer in the far corner; a faint sickly yellow-green line ghosts along the wall. A narrow amber band of dawn on the horizon, indigo and teal night sky washes. No text, no writing anywhere; all tags and papers are blank. Wide landscape composition.
