Risograph print in three flat ink layers (teal, fluorescent orange, cream paper) with slight misregistration and halftone grain: a walnut table at first dawn light, seen at a low three-quarter angle. On it stands a small wooden pigeonhole post-office cabinet with thirty blank-faced compartments; a few compartments hold rolled paper scrolls, one holds a brass key. A single fluorescent-pink thread runs from the key across the table to a small cocked brass primer in the far corner. A thin sickly yellow-green thread ghosts along the table edge. At the cabinet's side a tiny paper figure with a brass lever rests its head on a closed toll gate, asleep. All paper tags are blank. Quiet, waiting mood, dark teal shadows with one warm orange dawn beam from the left. No text, no lettering.
