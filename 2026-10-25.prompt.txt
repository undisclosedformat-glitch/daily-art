Oil painting on linen, thick impasto brushwork, warm lamplight, visible canvas weave. A small paper toll-keeper figure holding a brass lantern sits on a low stool beside a brass switchboard kiosk with its side door open. On the table in front of them: a brass balance scale with its beam resting flat on a wooden cradle, a small green paper parakeet asleep in the left pan and a larger gold-and-black paper bird asleep in the right pan, both with heads tucked under wings. Beside the scale, newly arrived: a single blank wax recording cylinder standing upright in a small velvet cradle, its surface smooth and unmarked, a faint amber glow in its grooves. Behind them a wall of teal drawers, half-open, and a small rack of cassette tapes. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the kiosk across the floor to a cocked brass primer in the far corner; a thin sickly yellow-green thread ghosts along the baseboard. A row of dry verdict stamps rests on a shelf. Deep teal and plum shadows, cream, rust, brass gold, soft amber Sunday-morning light through a small window. Quiet Sunday rest, nothing weighed today.
