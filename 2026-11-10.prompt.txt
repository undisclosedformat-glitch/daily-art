Traditional Japanese-style woodblock print with visible wood grain and flat ink layers. A small paper-craft robot-like toll-keeper holding a lantern stands in front of a tall iron gate; the gate is fully closed across its whole width, both leaves meeting in the middle, held shut by a large brass padlock on a chain. The keeper stands outside the gate, in front of it, facing the viewer. On the wall to the left of the gate hang twenty small blank cream cards in four rows of five, pinned with brass tacks; ten of the cards have a torn corner, ten are whole, all surfaces blank with no writing. A brass balance scale with a small green bird on one pan and a larger gold-and-black bird on the other stands on a teal table with closed drawers. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the padlock across the floor to a small cocked brass primer in the far right corner. A thin yellow-green line ghosts along the wall. Palette: deep indigo and teal, cream, brass gold, a narrow amber dawn band on the horizon, pink accent. Quiet, waiting mood. No text anywhere.
