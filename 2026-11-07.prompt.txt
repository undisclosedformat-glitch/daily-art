Charcoal and white chalk drawing on mid-gray toned paper, soft smudged blacks with chalk highlights and visible paper grain, one amber pastel band as the only strong color. A small paper toll-keeper figure holding a lantern stands before a shut iron gate with a brass padlock at pre-dawn. Newly added: on a stone slab beside the gate sits a leaning stack of seven small brown-paper parcels tied with twine, each with a blank cream tag, the top parcel slightly open with a few loose blank sheets spilling out, as if a sorting job was started and left. A single amber dawn band glows along the horizon. Beside the figure a brass balance scale at rest holds a small green bird on one pan and a larger gold-and-black bird on the other. A fluorescent-pink thread runs from the lantern to the padlock and off toward a small cocked brass primer in the far corner. A thin sickly yellow-green line ghosts along the gate bars. Closed teal drawers line the wall behind. Quiet, waiting Saturday mood, charcoal gray and cream with brass gold, teal and pink accents. No readable text anywhere; every tag and paper is blank.
