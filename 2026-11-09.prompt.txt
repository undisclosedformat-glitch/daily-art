Easel day: a traditional oil painting, thick visible brushwork, of a paper toll-keeper figure with a lantern standing at a big wooden easel, painting the very scene it stands in. On the easel canvas is a smaller wet copy of the same picture: a shut iron gate with a brass padlock, and a brass balance scale holding a small green bird and a larger gold-and-black bird. In the real scene, a shut iron gate with a brass padlock stands behind the keeper, the same brass balance scale beside it, and a fluorescent-pink thread running from the padlock across the floor to a cocked brass primer in the far corner. On a small table beside the easel stands a brass card-catalog cabinet of tiny drawers, one drawer pulled open with index cards fanning out like a hand of cards, its front brass label reads exactly "FIND" in clear capital letters. The keeper's brush is dipped in teal paint. All other tags and papers are blank. Deep indigo and teal shadows, cream highlights, brass gold, a thin amber dawn band at the window, pink accent. Quiet Monday stillness, work waiting.
