Cyanotype print on handmade paper with hand-tinted touches of gold and pink: a Monday morning on a ridge. A small paper toll-keeper figure in a trench coat stands holding a brass lantern, facing a brass switchboard kiosk of plugged cords. The kiosk's small side door, which was shut all week, now stands ajar with warm amber light spilling out, and a single new brass key still sits in its lock. Beside the kiosk a wooden rack of four verdict stamps with dry cream-coloured ink pads, untouched. Wooden bins of brass keys, a folded paper boat and film spools stand nearby. A fluorescent-pink thread runs along the ground from a brass key to a cocked brass primer in the far corner; a faint sickly yellow-green thread lies near the kiosk base. Behind them a pale gold sun rises over layered Prussian-blue ridges and mist. All paper tags, stamps and surfaces are blank, no writing anywhere. Quiet Monday stillness, deep cyan-blue shadows, cream whites, brass gold, one pink accent.
