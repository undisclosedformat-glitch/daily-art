Sumi-e ink wash painting on pale silk, horizontal scroll composition: a small paper toll-keeper figure holding a brass lantern stands on a rocky ridge at first light beside a tall brass switchboard kiosk; from the kiosk four cords run down to four small wooden bins labelled only with blank paper tags, each bin holding a different heap (brass keys, seed packets, a paper boat, a film spool) as if verdicts are about to be dropped in; a single fluorescent-pink thread runs from a key in the nearest bin across the ridge to a cocked brass primer in the far corner; a thin sickly yellow-green thread trails at the kiosk base. Loose grey and black ink washes of mist, deep teal wash in the valley, one warm amber dawn glow behind the ridge, brass gold highlights. No readable text anywhere; tags are blank.
